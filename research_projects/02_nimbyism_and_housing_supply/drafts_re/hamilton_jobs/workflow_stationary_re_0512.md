# Stationary Old-Model RE Benchmarks 2026-05-12

This packet solves stationary rational-expectations benchmark objects on the old-paper source file.

Interpretation:

- These are not full transition-path RE results.
- In a stationary benchmark, rational expectations means households correctly forecast the constant future price and policy environment.
- A one-off baby boom has no stationary counterpart. The closest stationary analogue is a permanently younger age structure, implemented here as positive cohort-growth age weights.
- Secular ageing is represented two ways: a negative cohort-growth stationary age profile and official projection age distributions for 2050 and 2100.

Rows are run at pass-throughs `0.006` and `0.012`.

Main output:

`truth/stationary_re_0512/stationary_re_ranked_0512.csv`

The table reports the terminal fixed-point price, gap, political pressure/restriction, homeownership, housing demand, and price change relative to the baseline stationary row at the same pass-through.
