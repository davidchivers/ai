## Bottom line

The draft is largely consistent after the UK-English pass. Remaining high-value issues are a small set of visible grammar/typo fixes, a few `impact`/AI-like phrases, and two places where the 15 per cent result would read better as a model-implied accounting result rather than a broad causal statement. I found no generic use of `benchmark`; the manuscript uses specific labels such as `current-price rule`, which is preferable.
