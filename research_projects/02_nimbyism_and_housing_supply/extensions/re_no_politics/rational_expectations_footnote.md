# Rational Expectations Footnote

## Recommended placement

Insert as a footnote on the first sentence that imposes the random-walk expectation assumption in
the main text.

## Exact footnote text

We explored a stripped-down rational-expectations transition version of the model in which
households form model-consistent expectations over future house prices. The resulting transition is
close to the benchmark-style path, suggesting that the random-walk forecasting shortcut is unlikely
to be central to the paper's qualitative conclusions. Solving the full political
rational-expectations transition would, however, require a fixed point over the entire future path
of prices and endogenous political support, which is substantially more computationally demanding.
We therefore retain the benchmark expectations structure in the main text and leave a full
political rational-expectations treatment to future work.
