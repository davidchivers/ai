# Business applications recession windows

Annual sums of Census Business Formation Statistics from the flow-side companion series.

## 2007-2010 Great Recession window

- `Total applications`: 2,652,392 -> 2,497,405 (-5.84%)
- `High-propensity applications`: 1,493,428 -> 1,163,075 (-22.12%)
- `High-propensity share`: 56.30% -> 46.57% (-9.73 percentage points)

## 2019-2022 pandemic window

- `Total applications`: 3,517,607 -> 5,083,802 (+44.52%)
- `High-propensity applications`: 1,322,307 -> 1,731,570 (+30.95%)
- `High-propensity share`: 37.59% -> 34.06% (-3.53 percentage points)
