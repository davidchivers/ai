# Owner question bank

## A. Business model

- Which products should be sold online versus only shown or only sold in person?
- Should all bottled wines be potentially available for collection, or only selected ranges?
- Is local delivery the only delivery model, or is wider shipping planned?
- Should informational wine listings be used to support discovery even when buying is disabled?
- Should gift vouchers, events, and private hire stay as separate Shopify-native products?

## B. Stock and operations

- What system currently tracks stock?
- Is there already a POS or management platform in use?
- How are by-the-glass pours currently tracked?
- How often does stock change during service?
- Who updates stock and how often?
- Is bottle stock counted daily, weekly, or only when problems appear?
- Should opening a bottle for service immediately reduce stock by one?

## C. Website and merchandising

- Should the site prioritise shop, wine bar, or equal hybrid positioning?
- Should Daniel’s notes be public, private, or both in different forms?
- Which filters matter most to customers in practice?
- Should some wines be visible online even when not purchasable?
- Which products or categories deserve homepage priority?
- Does the owner want a more editorial tone, a more practical retail tone, or a balance?

## D. Fulfilment

- What are the exact local delivery rules?
- Which postcodes or radius are supported?
- Which days and cutoff times apply for delivery?
- Which products are collection only?
- How should out-of-stock or low-stock items behave online?
- Should some products stay visible but disable purchase when stock is low?

## E. Media

- Do suppliers provide usable bottle or label images?
- Does the owner want to upload images manually?
- Is consistency of photography important enough to justify a standard image workflow?
- Who approves imagery before a product goes live?
- Is Daniel happy using supplier label art when custom photography is unavailable?

## F. Future integrations

- What management software is already used?
- Is Gmail or another inbox used for supplier orders?
- Is there appetite to invest in automation later if version 1 works?
- Is POS integration a real next-step priority or just a future possibility?
- Would low-stock alerts and reorder suggestions be valuable enough to justify a second-phase build?
