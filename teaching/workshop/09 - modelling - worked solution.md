# AI vs human production function

<div style="font-size: 1.4em;">

## Variables

$$
\begin{aligned}
Y   &:\ \text{output} \\
H   &:\ \text{human labour} \\
AI  &:\ \text{AI input} \\
A   &>\ 0 \quad \text{: total factor productivity} \\
\alpha &\in (0,1) \quad \text{: human-labour share}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Production function

$$
Y = A H^{\alpha} AI^{1-\alpha}
$$
